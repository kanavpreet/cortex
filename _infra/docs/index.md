# 🤖 Matik - AI-Powered Operations Platform

Welcome to **Matik** - Airbnb's next-generation AI-powered operations platform designed to revolutionize how we manage technical operations and operational data.

---

## 📖 Introduction

**Matik** (derived from the Tagalog word "awtomatiko" meaning automatic) is Airbnb's ambitious initiative to advance our technical operations capabilities through artificial intelligence and automation. This platform represents our commitment to creating a more proactive, intelligent, and highly automated operational culture across all engineering domains.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

Matik is an AI-powered operations platform that centralizes, analyzes, and processes operational data to help engineering and operations teams manage the growing complexity and volume of our technical infrastructure.

### 🎯 Primary Goals:
- **Reduce Manual Operations**: Lower the manual workload for engineering and operations staff
- **Centralize Operational Data**: Consolidate disparate operational data into actionable insights
- **Advance AI Ops**: Leverage AI for predictive insights and autonomous incident resolution
- **Transform Operations**: Create a proactive, intelligent operational culture over the next five years

### ⚡ Key Features:
- 📊 **Data Catalog API** for structured, searchable operational data
- 🔗 **System Integration** (Freyja, SPOG, REMAP) for centralized monitoring
- 🧠 **AI-powered Runbooks** and root cause analysis
- 🚨 **Automated Alert Catalog** with actionable insights
- 📈 **Interactive Dashboards** and chatbot interfaces
- 🤖 **Predictive Analytics** for proactive issue resolution

### 🗓️ Timeline:
- **MVP Release**: Q3 2025
- **Phased Rollouts**: Through Q1 2026
- **Continuous Expansion**: Ongoing AI capability enhancements
